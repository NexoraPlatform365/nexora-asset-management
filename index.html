Help centre

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers for visitors who are learning about Nexora and the investment process.

What assets can I explore?

The website covers real estate, gold and precious metals, oil and energy, digital assets, private markets, infrastructure and alternatives. Specific investable products must be listed separately with approved terms.

Are the charts showing Nexora's returns?

No. The charts show third-party public market benchmarks. They do not represent Nexora portfolio performance or predict future returns.

How do I get information about a current opportunity?

Use the contact form or email the admin. Ask for the official offering documents, fees, eligibility rules, valuation method and exit terms.

Can I deposit cryptocurrency right now?

Contact the admin first. Confirm the official receiving address, exact network, account reference and whether the authorised operator is accepting deposits.

Does creating an enquiry open an investor account?

This current website is a frontend prototype. The registration form records no data and does not create a secure account. Secure account services require a connected backend.

How are investment returns calculated?

Returns must be calculated from the documented terms and actual verified results of the relevant investment, with fees and losses accounted for. No return is guaranteed by this website.
