Help centre

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers for visitors who are learning about Nexora and the investment process.

What assets can I explore?

Explore the asset classes featured on our website, then contact our team for current opportunity details, eligibility and the steps to proceed.

Are the charts showing Nexora's returns?

No. The charts show public market benchmarks for general information. They do not represent Nexora portfolio performance or predict future returns.

How do I get information about a current opportunity?

Email our team and request current availability, an overview of the opportunity, eligibility details, relevant documents and guidance through the next steps.

Can I deposit cryptocurrency right now?

Contact the team for the approved process and instructions. Always confirm the recipient, network and terms directly through an official company channel before transferring any funds.

Does creating an enquiry open an investor account?

Creating an account gives you access to your profile. To discuss a specific investment opportunity, contact our team for current information and the next steps.

How are investment returns calculated?

Returns must be calculated from the documented terms and actual verified results of the relevant investment, with fees and losses accounted for. No return is guaranteed by this website.
